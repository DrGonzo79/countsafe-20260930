# Office Hours: product diagnostic

**Mode:** startup diagnostic · **Stage:** pre-product · **Position:** the reconciliation service is credible only if it sells reduced month-end anxiety and evidence quality, not vague “AI compliance.”

## Six forcing questions

1. **Demand reality:** evidence is market/problem evidence, not customer demand. Enforcement attention and historical settlements show consequence, but no clinic has paid for this product. The first milestone is three paid manual reconciliations.
2. **Status quo:** a practice manager or controlled-substance coordinator compares paper administration/waste logs, invoices, transfers, and a physical count in a spreadsheet; discrepancies trigger recounts and searching through binders.
3. **Desperate specificity:** the initial user is the practice manager at an independent 2–8 veterinarian clinic who owns month-end records and risks a failed inspection, owner escalation, or patient-care disruption.
4. **Narrowest paid wedge:** $249 per clinic/month for one human-reviewed monthly reconciliation packet covering up to ten controlled substances. No autonomous compliance claims.
5. **Observation:** watch five managers close a month without guidance. Record time-to-first-variance, evidence hunting, correction patterns, and where they ask another employee for help.
6. **Future-fit:** electronic logs and AI extraction make ingest easier, but fragmented vendors and higher expectations make a neutral evidence/reconciliation layer more essential—if it preserves provenance.

## Premise challenge and approaches

- **Manual service (S/low):** spreadsheet plus reviewer. Fastest demand test; limited scale.
- **Human-in-the-loop workspace (M/medium, recommended):** deterministic reconciliation first, extraction suggestions second, immutable evidence. Balances speed and trust.
- **Autonomous compliance platform (XL/high):** integrations and automated filings. Broad but premature and dangerous.

The prototype implements the middle architecture's smallest vertical slice. The assignment is to sell and perform three manual month-end closes before building OCR.
