## Useful source summary

The disclosed thesis is that veterinary practices face more frequent controlled-substance scrutiny while many still reconcile paper logs and purchase invoices manually. The proposed service receives source records, matches purchases against usage, produces a clean reconciliation, and packages evidence for audit response.

## Product interpretation

CountSafe narrows that broad service to one defensible workflow: a responsible clinic employee enters confirmed monthly totals for one drug, reviews the exact expected-versus-observed variance, and prepares a documented exception checklist. AI photo extraction is future work and must never silently post to the legal record.
